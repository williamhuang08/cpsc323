*** THINGS TO REMEMBER ***
In instructions
q - 64 bits
l - 32 bits
w - 16 bits
b - 8 bits

Examples:
%rax = R[rax] = get the value stored in the register rax
(%eax) = M[R[%eax]] = take the value at the register eax as an address, get the value at that address
7(%eax, ecx) = M[7 + R[%eax] + R[%ecx]]
7(%eax, %ecx, 4) = M[7 + R[%eax] + R[%ecx] * 4]
